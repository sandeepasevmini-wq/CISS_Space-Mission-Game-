1. Game Modes & Crew Operations

Understand the difference between Competitive Missions, Sandbox Testing, Solo Flight, and 4-Player Team Mode.

🏆 Competitive Mode Ranked

The official mission simulation where every dollar, kilogram, and engineering decision counts toward your rank.

  • Strict Constraints: You must stay strictly within your difficulty tier's budget cap ($750M–$1500M) and rocket payload mass limit (1,500 kg or 3,000 kg).
  • Real Consequences: Under-equipping power, shielding, or bandwidth causes mission failure upon arrival.
  • Global Leaderboard Eligible: Successful missions can be uploaded with your Callsign and Country Flag to compete worldwide.
  • Score Optimization: Unused budget converts directly into bonus score points!

🧪 Sandbox Mode Free Lab

An open engineering playground designed for experimentation, learning subsystem trade-offs, and testing wild spacecraft builds.

  • Pressure-Free Testing: Experiment with heavy SLS rockets, Laser Comms, and maximum radiation shielding without worrying about leaderboard penalties.
  • 3D Hangar Inspection: Visually see how every part changes your spacecraft's 3D geometry in real time.
  • Trajectory & Telemetry Practice: Test how Direct vs. Gravity Assist routes affect transit speed and solar power decay over distance.
  • Great for Training: Use Sandbox to prototype designs before taking them into Architect difficulty in Competitive Mode.

👥 Solo Commander vs. 4-Player Team Mode Live Co-Op

Play solo with full control over all systems, or Host/Join a 4-digit room code with friends where each player controls specific subsystems:

🎖️ Commander (CMD) — Controls Target, Difficulty & Launch Vehicle. Bonus goal: Keep at least $150M unspent.

⚙️ Lead Engineer (ENG) — Controls Power System & Radiation Shielding. Bonus goal: Keep total mass ≤ 1,000 kg.
🔬 Chief Scientist (SCI) — Controls Target & Science Instrument. Bonus goal: Equip Radar or Spectrometer.

📡 Flight Director (FD) — Controls Flight Route & Comms Antenna. Bonus goal: Achieve ≥ 500 kbps uplink.
💡 Team Synergy Bonus: Meeting all 4 crew role goals awards +500 PTS! Meeting 2–3 awards +150 PTS, while meeting 0–1 incurs a -200 PTS penalty.

4. Difficulty Tiers & Mid-Flight Anomalies

Higher difficulty levels shrink your budget and change how mid-flight space emergencies behave.

🟢 Cadet (Easy) $1,500M

  • Generous Budget: $1,500M allows you to afford top-tier parts easily.
  • Smooth Transit: No mid-flight anomalies occur during your journey.
  • Ideal for first-time pilots learning orbital mechanics.

🟡 Commander (Medium) $1,000M

  • Balanced Budget: $1,000M requires smart trade-offs between cost and performance.
  • Tactical Pause Anomalies: Around halfway through transit, an emergency alert triggers and pauses your spacecraft until you choose a response protocol.

🔴 Architect (Hard) $750M

  • Tight Budget: Only $750M—requires Gravity Assist or lean engineering.
  • Real-Time Anomalies: Transit does NOT pause during an emergency! React before reaching the target or take a -200 PTS penalty.

5. Scoring Formula, Reports & Mission Comparison

How your final Mission Grade (S, A, B, C, or F) is calculated and how to reach the top of the leaderboard.

📊 How Points & Grades Work

  • Base Science Score: Instrument Points × 10 × Data Efficiency (up to 1,200 PTS with Subsurface Radar + HGA/Laser Comms).
  • Budget Efficiency Bonus: Every $1M unspent adds +1 PT directly to your final score!
  • Grade Thresholds:
    S-Grade > 1,200 PTS  |  A-Grade > 900 PTS  |  B-Grade Passing

📑 Post-Mission Tools

  • Official CISS Mission Report: Generates a downloadable engineering debrief explaining why your mission succeeded or failed, with tailored upgrade advice.
  • Compare Missions (`compare.html`): Archives your runs so you can compare two spacecraft blueprints side-by-side.
  • Global Leaderboard: Filter top scores by destination and difficulty tier, and submit your personal best!
