📖 HOW TO PLAY: MISSION BRIEFING

Scroll through the complete briefing to unlock mission acceptance.

1. MISSION OBJECTIVES & DESTINATIONS

Select your destination wisely between Europa (Jupiter), Titan (Saturn), or 16 Psyche (Asteroid Belt). Each moon or asteroid imposes distinct engineering hurdles, such as intense Jovian radiation belts, cryogenic nitrogen atmospheres, or metallic core magnetic anomalies.

2. DEPLOYMENT MODES

Choose between Solo Flight or Team Mode (supporting a maximum of 4 players). In Team Mode, synchronize live with your crew across devices; crew roles automatically adapt to 1, 2, 3, or 4 logged-in players:

  • Commander (CMD): Budget credits, difficulty tier, and launch vehicle control.
  • Lead Engineer (ENG): Power generators (Solar vs. RTG) and radiation shielding.
  • Chief Scientist (SCI): Target destination and scientific payload instruments.
  • Flight Director (FD): Interplanetary route trajectory and communications antenna.

3. RESOURCE MANAGEMENT & TRADE-OFFS

Every engineering choice has consequences. Upgrading instruments or adding shielding increases payload mass and budget costs. Ensure your spacecraft stays under mass limits and within your selected difficulty budget ($1,500M Cadet, $1,000M Commander, or $750M Architect).

4. 3D HANGAR & INTERPLANETARY TRANSIT

Inspect your spacecraft in real time using the Realistic 3D WebGL Hangar (click and drag to rotate). Monitor real-time telemetry, power draws, data throughput, and fuel consumption along the Mission Viz Interplanetary Trajectory Map during transit.

5. MID-FLIGHT ANOMALIES & DEBRIEFS

Handle destination-specific emergency anomalies mid-flight by coordinating tactical choices with your team. Complete missions to generate diagnostic optimization reports, transmission records for the Global Hall of Fame, and shareable comparisons (compare.html).
